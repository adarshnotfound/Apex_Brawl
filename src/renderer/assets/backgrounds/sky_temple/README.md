# Mystic Combat — The Eternal Sky Temple Environmental Assets Package

This archive contains the 13 coordinated, high-resolution (1920x1080) environmental assets with true alpha transparency for **Mystic Combat** (2D side-view fighting game in Phaser.js and Electron).

## Included Assets
1. `01_static_background_sky.png`: Clean celestial sky and boundless cloud ocean abyss (un-occluded, zero platform artifacts).
2. `02_distant_floating_islands.png`: Far floating island pinnacles and mist-covered peaks.
3. `03_distant_architectural_structures.png`: Suspended viaduct bridges, cliffside pagodas, and distant stone arches.
4. `04_midground_towers_and_cliffs.png`: Midground temples, vertical rock faces, and cliff sanctuaries.
5. `05_large_foreground_pillars.png`: Colossal carved stone pillars rising beyond the upper frame.
6. `06_floating_inverted_rock_formations.png`: Enormous inverted mountain monoliths descending from overhead clouds.
7. `07_separate_cloud_layers.png`: Multi-depth volumetric cloud puffs and horizontal drift sheets.
8. `08_transparent_mist_textures.png`: Vertical chasm thermals and rising waterfall mist sprites.
9. `09_waterfall_textures.png`: Cascading water stream textures and rock splash particle sprites.
10. `10_atmospheric_particle_sprites.png`: Floating rock fragments, sunbeam dust motes, leaves, and cyan magical motes.
11. `11_light_ray_overlays.png`: Volumetric cyan and soft white sunlight god-rays with smooth additive gradients.
12. `12_foreground_atmospheric_effects.png`: Foreground abyss cloud wisps and bottom stage edge fog.
13. `13_main_fighting_platform.png`: The horizontal weathered stone fighting platform slab, ancient carvings, damp reflective stone patches, ruined side markers, and colossal supporting pillars with 100% genuine transparency (zero background clouds baked in).
